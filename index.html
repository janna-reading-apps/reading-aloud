Read aloud

Press Start reading, allow microphone access, then read the text from beginning to end. Press Stop and check when you finish.

Practice only, not a pronunciation grade. Speech recognition may mishear accents, names and background noise. Your browser may send audio to its speech service. Internet is usually needed. Use with your teacher's or parent's permission. No student account is needed.

Browser support varies by device. Try a supported desktop or Android version of Chrome or Edge. If you opened this in a messaging app, open it in your browser instead.
